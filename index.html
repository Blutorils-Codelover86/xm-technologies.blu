The XM Lab

MOST THINGS START AS QUESTIONS.

What if a codebase could be explored like a map? What if an AI assistant could feel more like part of a computer? What happens when computer vision meets environmental problems? What can a robot learn from the space around it? XM Technologies exists to explore those questions by building.

Building independently

BUILT WITHOUT A BLUEPRINT.

XM Technologies is currently an independent, founder-led technology studio. Projects are designed, developed, broken and rebuilt through hands-on experimentation. There are no large teams behind the interface. Just curiosity, code, hardware and an unreasonable number of ideas.

About

WHO IS XM?

XM Technologies was founded by Vivaan Shandilya as an independent technology studio and home for ambitious experiments. The studio brings together interests in artificial intelligence, software, robotics, computer vision and emerging computing systems. Some projects become prototypes. Some become long-term experiments. Some fail completely. All of them teach something.
